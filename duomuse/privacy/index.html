Fold Muse Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026.

Fold Muse does not require an account and does not display ads. Favorites, reading positions and Studio edits are stored on your device. Saving or sharing an image sends it only to the destination you choose through iOS.

Anonymous analytics

Fold Muse uses TelemetryDeck to understand how the app is used. Signals include artwork opened, saved or shared; favorites; screen usage; wallpaper-help and rating events; paywall views; and purchase events with a product identifier and result. TelemetryDeck also sends an anonymous installation identifier, a rounded timestamp and technical metadata such as app version, system version and device model. Its SDK declares Device ID and Product Interaction; Fold Muse also declares Purchase History for the anonymous purchase events. They are used for analytics, are not linked to your identity, and are not used for tracking across other companies' apps or websites. We do not send your name, email address, Apple Account, photos, exported filenames or Studio adjustments in these signals.

Apple handles payment and App Store account information through StoreKit. Fold Muse does not receive your payment-card details. Anonymous purchase events tell us which product was involved and whether the action completed, failed or was cancelled.

Artwork and downloads

Included previews and downloaded artwork remain on your device. Premium artwork files are delivered through Apple's hosted asset packs. Apple's services handle those requests under Apple's privacy practices. The new-edition check requests public App Store version information and does not send your artwork choices or edits.

Photos and sharing

Photo-library add permission is requested when you choose Save to Photos. Fold Muse does not read your personal photo library. If you choose iCloud or another destination when sharing, that service handles the exported file under its own terms. Exported files remain there until you remove them.

Retention and choices

You can remove favorites or saved edits in the app. Removing the app generally removes local app data, subject to iOS backups. Files exported elsewhere must be removed separately. TelemetryDeck's privacy information is available at https://telemetrydeck.com/privacy/ and https://telemetrydeck.com/docs/guides/privacy-faq/.

Support

Sending feedback starts an optional email draft. Only information you choose to send is included. We use support correspondence to respond to your request. You may request deletion of support correspondence through the support contact published at https://www.counttoday.com/duomuse/contact/.

Website

The support and privacy website is separate from the app. Its host may receive normal technical request information such as an IP address. Visiting the website does not give access to your favorites, photos or edits.

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date will identify the published revision.
