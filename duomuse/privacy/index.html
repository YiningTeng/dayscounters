Privacy Policy

Your art stays on your device.

Effective date: October 23, 2026

In short: Duo Muse is designed to work offline. No Duo Muse account is required. We do not operate an app backend, and the app does not include advertising, analytics or cross-app tracking. Your wallpaper selections, favorites and edits are handled on your iPhone, not sent to us.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Duo Muse iPhone app ("Duo Muse," "the app," "we," or "us") handles information. Duo Muse is an art wallpaper gallery and local customization studio designed for iPhone Duo.

2. Information stored on your device

The app may store wallpaper favorites, chosen designs, customization settings such as color and brightness, and other app preferences locally on your iPhone. Included wallpapers and the editing features are available offline. Duo Muse does not automatically transmit this information to us.

3. Photos, exports and sharing

When you choose to save or share a wallpaper, the image is rendered on your device and sent to the destination you select through iOS, such as Photos, Files or the system share sheet. Photo-library permission may be requested for saving, depending on the iOS export flow.

Duo Muse does not automatically upload wallpapers or your edits to a developer server. If you choose to save or share a file using iCloud or another third-party service, the destination service handles it under its own terms and privacy practices.

Images saved outside Duo Muse remain in the destination you selected, even if you later remove the app.

4. Purchases

If you purchase optional paid features, Apple processes payments and handles App Store account information. Duo Muse may use Apple's StoreKit framework to determine access to purchased features. We do not receive your payment-card details or operate a purchase-history collection server. Apple's own terms and privacy policy apply to those services.

5. Advertising, analytics and tracking

Duo Muse does not contain advertising, third-party analytics or cross-app tracking. We do not sell personal information or receive your local wallpaper preferences through the app.

6. Data retention and deletion

Local favorites and editing preferences are kept on your device until you change or remove them. Deleting the app generally removes its local app data, subject to iOS behavior and any device backups. Files you exported outside the app must be removed separately from their destinations.

7. Support email

If you contact support, we receive your email address and whatever details you choose to send. We use this information to respond to your request and keep it only as reasonably needed for support or applicable obligations. You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing us. Email services may process these messages under their own policies.

8. Informational website pages

The support and privacy pages on CountToday.com are separate from the offline app. The third-party service hosting these pages may receive routine technical information, such as IP addresses and HTTP requests, in accordance with its own practices. Merely visiting these pages does not give us access to your Duo Muse favorites or edits.

9. Children's privacy

Duo Muse is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

10. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy if Duo Muse changes, including if optional online content delivery is introduced in a future version. The effective date above will be updated to reflect the latest policy.

Contact

For privacy questions, email counttoday.contact@gmail.com, or visit Duo Muse Support.
