Support & Contact

How can we help?

Questions, feedback or a problem with Duo Muse? We'd love to hear from you.

Email Duo Muse Support

Send an email

counttoday.contact@gmail.com

What we can help with

  • Browsing art styles, collections and paired wallpapers
  • Inner and outer screen previews and fold preview
  • Color, brightness and crop customization
  • Saving wallpapers to Photos or Files
  • Purchases, restoring access and troubleshooting
  • New art requests, feature ideas and general feedback

Helpful details to include

For technical issues, please include your iPhone model, iOS version, Duo Muse app version, and the steps to reproduce the issue. If relevant, mention whether the issue occurs on the inner or outer screen. A screenshot can help, but please remove any personal information first.

Privacy

The app's included wallpapers, favorites and customization features work on your device. No Duo Muse account is needed. Please avoid sending private photos, passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information by email.

Read the Duo Muse Privacy Policy for more details.
