Met Muse

Art for your screen.
Stories to stay for.

Met Muse is a curated iPhone wallpaper gallery and reading room. Discover artwork, explore the stories behind it, frame your wallpaper and save it to Photos.

Make it yours

Browse complimentary and Pro works, save favorites, read artist introductions and illustrated stories, and adjust framing and light in the Studio. Some high-resolution artwork and motion previews are downloaded through Apple’s content-delivery services.

Motion previews are demonstrations inside the app. Wallpaper installation is completed using iOS; Met Muse does not control system wallpaper animations.
