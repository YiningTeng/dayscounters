Support

Effective date: November 16, 2026

Contact Met Muse

For help, feedback, purchase assistance or content corrections, email batterseaworks@gmail.com. Include the app version, iPhone model, iOS version and a short description. Screenshots are optional; remove personal information before sending.

Set a wallpaper

Open an artwork, adjust framing and save to Photos. In Photos, select the image and choose Share → Use as Wallpaper. Options vary by iOS version.

Restore Pro

Open Pro and choose Restore using the Apple Account used to purchase. Check your connection and allow artwork downloads to finish. Met Muse and Fold Muse have separate purchase entitlements.

Subscriptions and refunds

Manage subscriptions in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Apple processes billing and refunds; visit reportaproblem.apple.com for refund requests.
