Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

Your collection stays yours

Met Muse requires no account and includes no advertising, third-party analytics or cross-app tracking. We do not operate a server collecting your wallpaper choices, favorites, reading history or edits.

On-device information

Favorites, reading progress, editing recipes, app preferences and usage milestones used to time optional review prompts are stored locally, not transmitted to us. Your iOS backup settings may include app data.

Apple services and downloads

Some high-resolution artwork and motion previews are downloaded through Apple-hosted asset packs. Downloads require a connection, and Apple may process technical information needed for delivery. Apple handles payments, subscriptions, refunds and App Store accounts. The app uses verified StoreKit purchase information to enable Pro and restore access. We do not receive payment-card details or operate a purchase-history collection server. Apple’s privacy practices apply to its services.

Photos, exports and sharing

When you choose to save a wallpaper, the app requests permission to add images to Photos. It does not need to read your personal photo library for this purpose. Images are rendered on-device. Files, iCloud and other destinations you choose handle exported files under their own policies. Exported images remain outside the app until you remove them.

Support correspondence

If you send feedback or email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to provide, such as an issue description or app version. We use this to respond and retain it only as reasonably needed for support or applicable obligations. Avoid sending sensitive personal information. You may request deletion of support correspondence.

Retention and deletion

Change preferences or remove favorites in the app. Deleting the app generally removes local data, subject to iOS and backup behavior. It does not cancel a subscription or delete exported files. Manage subscriptions in Apple Account settings.

Website and third parties

GitHub Pages hosts these pages and may receive routine request information, including IP addresses. These pages add no advertising cookies or analytics scripts. External websites, email providers and Apple have their own policies. Visiting this website does not expose your app collection to us.

Children and changes

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. Contact us if a child has provided personal information through support. We may update this policy if features or practices change and will revise the effective date.
